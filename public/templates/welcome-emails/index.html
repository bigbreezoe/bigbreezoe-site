What's in it

  1. Email 1, right away. Deliver the thing they signed up for, with one next step.
  2. Email 2, day 2. The step most people skip, and why it's worth doing first.
  3. Email 3, day 5. Before you buy anything: three questions to ask yourself.
  4. Email 4, day 9. Ask what they're stuck on, and invite a reply.

Each email does one job and points to one next step. Every blank is marked, so you can fill it in with how you'd answer the question out loud.

The 3 email rules from the episode

  1. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works.
  2. Every email has your mailing address, or a P.O. box.
  3. Honor an unsubscribe within 10 business days.

These come from the FTC's guide to the CAN-SPAM Act, which says each separate email that breaks the rules can cost up to $53,088 in penalties. That's general information, not legal advice. Read the FTC guide yourself.
