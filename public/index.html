Questions people ask first

Straight answers, so the intake is the easy part.

Do I pay before I apply?

Not for the packages. Getting Started, The Basics and The Full Playbook start with the intake, and if it's a fit I reply with times and a payment link. Nobody is charged for a session I haven't agreed to give. A single service with a Pay and schedule button is different: you pay up front, then pick your time.

What if it's not a fit?

I'll tell you honestly and point you to what will help more, usually the free blueprint and the episodes. You won't be sold something you don't need.

Will this make me money?

I can't promise that, and nobody honest can. I teach, set up and guide. Results depend on the work you do and the time you have. Nothing here is legal, tax or financial advice.

Who is this not for?

Anyone looking for guaranteed income or a shortcut, or anyone who wants me to run their accounts. I work in your tools by invitation only, and I never ask for your passwords.

How does Big Breezoe make money?

Three ways, all on the Shop tab: coaching and setup sessions, digital products, and affiliate commissions when you buy a tool through one of my links. You pay the same price either way, and every link is labeled. The channel isn't monetized yet.

What I won't do: recommend something because it pays more, claim I used something I didn't, promise you'll make money, or hide that a link is an affiliate link.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, once, with 24 hours' notice. A session missed without notice counts as held. Cancel before the first session for a full refund. The full terms are on the terms page.

What happens to what I share?

It comes to me only, to decide whether I can help and to plan your session. See the privacy page for the details.
