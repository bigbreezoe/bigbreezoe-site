I only publish a guide after I've built that piece myself, so the ones marked "being built" follow my own build.

  1. Your site and free guide

    People can't hire you or follow you if they can't find you. Turn your preview into a live one-page site, with your own free guide people sign up for.

    The Road · built

  2. Your email list and welcome emails

    An app can lock you out overnight. Set up a list you own, a sign-up form and welcome emails that send on their own.

    The Road · built

  3. Your content and Shorts

    Film one video a week, cut it into Shorts and schedule them ahead, because nobody with a job can post every day.

    Content · built

  4. Your gear page

    People will ask what you use. Give them one page with tools in three price lanes, your own links and an honest label on each.

    Gear · built

  5. Your AI crew

    Set up your first AI helpers so the routine work stops eating your evenings. You stay the boss.

    The Crew · built

  6. Your shop

    A small shop page for your own service or your own picks, so people who want to pay you have somewhere to do it.

    The Shop · being built

  7. Your sound page

    Sleep, focus and wake-up tracks that keep you steady while you build.

    The Breeze · being built

  8. Your trading setup

    A chart tool, a journal and a risk plan. Tools and habits only. No trade calls and no promised returns.

    Markets · being built
