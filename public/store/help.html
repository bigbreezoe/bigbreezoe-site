What you get, step by step

Every package starts with a short application. If you're starting from zero, we go in the same order I did when I started Big Breezoe: your name and handles, a simple page, your own domain, a business email, your email list, and then the affiliate programs you want to join. Calls happen on a video call where you share your screen and do the clicking. If I need to take control of your screen for a moment, I ask first. I also ask before I record anything, and you always type your own passwords and payments.

Getting Started session, one 60-minute call

BEFORE THE CALL

  1. 01Fill in the short application. Tell me where you are and what feels stuck.
  2. 02I read it and tell you whether a session fits. If it does, I send you a private link to pay and pick a time.
  3. 03From your answers I pick the one step we'll work on, so the call starts on your problem. If you're starting from zero, that's usually your name and handles.

DURING THE CALL

  1. 04We meet for 60 minutes on a video call. You share your screen and click while I guide you through each part.
  2. 05By the end of the hour that one step works, and it's yours.

AFTER THE CALL

  1. 06You get a written recap of what we did and your next step.

The Basics, three calls and the build between them

BEFORE THE KICKOFF

  1. 01Fill in the application. Tell me what you want to build and how much you're willing to do yourself.
  2. 02If it fits, I send you a private link to pay. The Basics is paid in full before the kickoff, unless we agree a payment plan in writing.

DURING THE CALLS

  1. 03Kickoff call, 60 minutes. We set up your name and handles, your domain and your business email together, live. You click, I guide.
  2. 04Between calls, I build the loop that keeps you posting: a place to save ideas, a simple way to make each post, and a schedule that shares them for you.
  3. 05Review call, 30 minutes. I show you what I built, you try it, and we fix anything that feels off.
  4. 06Handoff call, 30 minutes. You run it yourself while I watch.

AFTER THE HANDOFF

  1. 07You get a written guide to everything we built, and you own all of it.
  2. 08For 14 days you can email me any question about the setup.

The Full Playbook, scoped to your business

BEFORE YOU PAY

  1. 01Fill in the application. Tell me about your business and where you want it to go.
  2. 02We agree in writing on what we'll build, how many calls it takes and the timeline. You pay only after that.

DURING THE WORK

  1. 03Kickoff call, 60 minutes. We start the same way as The Basics and set up your base together, live.
  2. 04We add the automation underneath, so the routine steps run without you doing each one by hand.
  3. 05We add tracking, so you can see which posts and links are actually working.

AFTER EVERY CALL

  1. 06You get written notes from that call. At the end, everything is documented so you can keep it running yourself.
