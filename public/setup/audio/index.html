Fix bad phone audio first

Bad phone audio is common, and most of the time you can fix it for free before you buy anything. Start with the free fixes below. Then decide if gear is worth it.

This page may include affiliate links. If you buy through them I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. I recommend things I use or things I researched and would consider, and I tell you which.

Free fixes first

  1. Pick a soft room over a hard room. A couch, a rug, curtains, or a closet full of clothes. Hard walls bounce your voice back.
  2. Close the door.
  3. Turn off fans, AC and the fridge if you can.
  4. Put the phone on airplane mode.
  5. Put the phone close to your mouth.
  6. Record a 10-second test and listen on headphones.
  7. Clap once at the start of every take.

If it sounds clear, you may not need to buy anything yet. That's a good answer.

Three questions before you buy

  1. Do you move around while filming?
  2. Is your room loud or echoey?
  3. Do you film far from the phone?

If all three answers are no, keep your money. The phone is enough.
